# The Constitution Of The United States Of America

This directory contains the text of the United States Constitution, organized
into the following structure:

- [`00-preamble.md`](00-preamble.md) — the Preamble.
- [`articles/`](articles/) — the seven Articles of the Constitution (plus
  Article VIII as it appears in the source text), each broken down by Section.
- [`amendments/`](amendments/) — the twenty-seven ratified Amendments.
